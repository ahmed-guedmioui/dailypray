How It Works

  • Choose Distractions: Select the apps, categories, and websites that pull you away most.
  • Automatic Locking: At prayer time, DailyPray locks them down.
  • Confirm & Unlock: Open the app, pray, then tap and hold to confirm. Everything unlocks and your streak goes up.

Accurate Times & Qibla

  • Accurate prayer times for your exact location
  • Live countdown front and centre
  • Friday midday prayer shown as Jumu'ah
  • Built-in Qibla compass pointing to the Kaaba

Wake Up for Fajr

Sleep inertia is real. Before the confirmation screen, DailyPray asks you to solve one small math sum. By the time you answer, you are genuinely awake.

Features Built For Consistency

  • Streaks & Rings: Track prayer and day streaks side by side with a 7-day ring system.
  • The Quran: All 114 surahs with Arabic text, translations, and full audio recitation.
  • Custom Call Options: Set unique alarms, adhan voices, or keep it quiet per prayer.
  • Private By Design: App blocking is handled securely via Apple’s Screen Time on your device. DailyPray never learns which apps you lock.
